Built for a Better Web

Sandarshana brings together cutting-edge privacy engineering and transparent analytics.

🛡️

Sandarshana Shields

High-performance network interception powered by curated EasyList & EasyPrivacy rules. Blocks telemetry, tracking pixels, and invasive ads at the network layer before they execute.

⏱️

Your Browsing Today

Signature active foreground time tracking using a monotonic hardware clock. Tracks real browsing time on domains and pauses automatically when in background or internal screens.

📊

Two-Level Data Insights

Honest network measurement: Level 1 measures exact UID bytes via Android TrafficStats, while Level 2 estimates per-site resource transfers with transparent disclosures.

🕶️

Private Session Isolation

Dedicated private mode tray with discrete visual identity. Zero persistence to normal history or time analytics, with complete cache and cookie clearing on tab exit.

📑

Multi-Tab Architecture

Fluid card-based tab switcher with instant tab duplication, search filtering, independent navigation stacks, and separate standard and private tab trays.

⚡

Zero Bloat & Zero Telemetry

No sign-in required, no crypto wallets, and no analytics sent to remote servers. All your bookmarks, history, and usage statistics remain strictly on your device.
