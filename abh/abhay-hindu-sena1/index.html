About the Founder

Radha Manohar Das

Founder of Abhay Hindu Sena, dedicated to guiding, protecting, and uniting Hindu society through structured social and religious initiatives.

The organization works across community outreach, cultural preservation, and public awareness — building an active network of volunteers committed to service and Dharma.
