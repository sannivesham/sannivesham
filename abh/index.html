🚩 Founder & National Convener

Shri Radha Manohar Das

Founder & National Convener — Abhay Hindu Sena

Shri Radha Manohar Das is a revered spiritual speaker, Vedic scholar, and passionate defender of Sanatana Dharma based in Tirupati. Having deeply studied the Bhagavad Gita and Vedic scriptures, and bringing valuable spiritual discipline from his time with ISKCON, he established Abhay Hindu Sena to awaken the youth against cultural complacency.

“A society that honors its temples, values its sacred traditions, and awakens its youth can never be defeated. Truth alone triumphs when courage leads.”

His pravachans emphasize moral character, physical courage, cow welfare, temple protection, and service to humanity. Through mass youth conventions and cultural outreach programs, he continues to inspire thousands to walk the path of Dharma fearlessly.

Our Sacred Pillars

Missions of Abhay Hindu Sena

We stand steadfast on five primary pillars of action to uplift society, safeguard temples, and empower future generations.

🛕

Temple & Land Protection

Vigilant defense against encroachment of ancient temple lands, restoration of neglected village shrines, and advocacy for traditional archaka welfare.

  • Anti-encroachment legal & social drives
  • Rural temple revival projects
  • Vedic rituals and archaka support
🚩

Youth Awakening & Defense

Instilling physical discipline, lathi training, self-defense tactics, and moral fortitude among youth to protect society and resist adversity.

  • Physical fitness & martial training camps
  • Leadership development workshops
  • Sanatana value camps for students
🍲

Nitya Annadanam & Seva

Serving hot, sanctified meals to pilgrims, underprivileged families, and community members during festivals and times of need.

  • Prasadam distribution at major pilgrim centers
  • Disaster relief food support
  • Nutritional support for rural children
🐄

Gau Samrakshana & Goshalas

Dedicated care, shelter, and medical support for indigenous Desi cows, continuous vigilance against illicit trafficking, and support for goshalas.

  • Active cow rescue & legal intervention
  • Fodder and medical camps for rural cattle
  • Promotion of Desi Panchagavya products
📚

Dharmic Literature & Education

Free distribution of Bhagavad Gita and sacred books, weekly satsangs, and digital media awareness to counter misinformation.

  • Bhagavad Gita drives in colleges & villages
  • Public satsangs & interactive Q&A
  • YouTube discourses for global reach
⚖️

Dharmic Legal Defense

Standing shoulder-to-shoulder with Hindus facing institutional neglect, providing legal aid, and defending sacred rituals and festivals.

  • Legal panel for temple dispute resolution
  • Protection of public religious celebrations
  • Rapid response volunteer network
