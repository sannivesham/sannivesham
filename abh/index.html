🚩 Founder & National Convener

Shri Radha Manohar Das

Founder & National Convener — Abhay Hindu Sena

Shri Radha Manohar Das is a revered spiritual speaker, Vedic scholar, and passionate defender of Sanatana Dharma based in Tirupati. Having deeply studied the Bhagavad Gita and Vedic scriptures, and bringing valuable spiritual discipline from his time with ISKCON, he established Abhay Hindu Sena to awaken youth and protect Hindu traditions.

“A society that honors its temples, values its sacred traditions, and awakens its youth can never be defeated. Truth alone triumphs when courage leads.”

His pravachans emphasize moral character, physical courage, cow welfare, temple protection, and service to humanity. Under his leadership, thousands of karyakarthas are actively safeguarding cultural heritage across two states.

Our Sacred Pillars

Core Missions of the Sena

Action-oriented initiatives for temple preservation, youth leadership, and humanitarian service.

🛕

Temple & Land Defense

Vigilant defense against encroachment on ancient temple lands, restoration of dilapidated rural mandirs, and welfare drives for traditional archakas.

🚩

Youth Awakening & Defense

Conducting martial discipline, physical fitness, and lathi training camps to empower youth with moral courage and self-defense skills.

🍲

Nitya Annadanam & Seva

Distributing free, sanctified meals to pilgrims, underprivileged families, and community members during major festivals and emergencies.

🐄

Gau Samrakshana & Goshalas

Protecting indigenous Desi cattle, providing medical and nutritional aid, combating illegal cow transport, and assisting rural goshalas.
