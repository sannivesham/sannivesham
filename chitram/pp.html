Privacy Policy for Chitram

Last Updated: September 27, 2026

Chitram ("we", "our", or "the app") is a GPS camera and photography application that allows users to capture photographs and optionally add location, date, time, and other information to their photographs.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Chitram accesses, how it is used, where it is stored, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Access

Chitram may request access to the following information to provide its core functionality.

Camera

Chitram requires camera access to allow you to take photographs.

Camera data is used only to provide camera and photography functionality. Chitram does not use the camera to continuously monitor you or record video/audio without your action.

Location Information

Chitram may access your device's location, including:

  • Latitude and longitude
  • Approximate or precise location, depending on the permission granted
  • Altitude, when available
  • Location accuracy
  • Speed or heading, when available
  • Location information used to determine a readable address

Location information is used to provide GPS information on photographs and related features.

Chitram does not sell your location information.

Chitram only requests location access when it is required for the app's location-related functionality.

Photos and Media

Chitram accesses photographs created or selected by you to provide photography, gallery, editing, saving, and sharing functionality.

Where supported by your device and Android version, Chitram uses system-provided photo/media selection mechanisms to minimize unnecessary access to your media library.

Device Information

Chitram may access limited technical information required for the app to function, such as:

  • Device model
  • Android version
  • Camera capabilities
  • Application version
  • Basic technical information required for compatibility

Chitram does not intentionally collect sensitive device identifiers such as IMEI numbers for advertising or tracking purposes.

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed by Chitram may be used to:

  • Capture photographs
  • Add GPS coordinates to photographs
  • Display location information
  • Add date and time information
  • Add altitude, accuracy, speed, or compass information when available
  • Generate GPS photo overlays
  • Display photographs in the Chitram gallery
  • Display photographs on a map when the map feature is used
  • Save photographs to your device
  • Allow you to share photographs
  • Improve the reliability and functionality of the application

We do not use your photographs or location information for purposes unrelated to the functionality described in this Privacy Policy.

3. Photo and GPS Information

When you choose to create a GPS-stamped photograph, location information may become part of the photograph itself.

For example, a photograph may contain:

  • Location/address
  • Latitude and longitude
  • Date
  • Time
  • Altitude
  • GPS accuracy
  • Other information selected by you

Once this information has been embedded into a photograph and you choose to save or share the photograph, it may be visible to anyone who receives or views that photograph.

You are responsible for reviewing the information displayed on a photograph before sharing it.

4. Data Storage

Chitram is designed to keep photographs and GPS information on your device unless a feature explicitly requires transmission to a third-party service.

Photographs created using Chitram may be stored in your device's storage or photo library according to your device settings and the permissions you grant.

We do not automatically upload your photographs to our servers.

5. Data Sharing

Chitram does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We do not share your photographs or precise location information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

If a feature requires a third-party service, such as map or address/geocoding functionality, the minimum information necessary for that feature may be transmitted to the relevant service.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Sharing Photos

Chitram may provide sharing functionality using the Android system sharing mechanism.

When you choose to share a photograph, the photograph and any GPS/location information embedded within it may be provided to the application or person you choose.

Chitram does not control how recipients subsequently use or redistribute photographs that you voluntarily share.

7. Permissions

Depending on the features you use, Chitram may request:

  • Camera permission — to take photographs
  • Location permission — to add GPS information
  • Photo/media permission or system photo picker access — to access photographs selected by you
  • Other permissions only when required for a specific feature

You can manage or revoke permissions through your Android device settings.

If you deny a permission, certain features that depend on that permission may not function.

8. Background Location

Chitram does not require continuous background location access for its core GPS camera functionality.

Location is intended to be accessed while you are actively using the relevant GPS camera or location-related feature.

If a future version introduces a feature requiring background location access, this Privacy Policy and the relevant in-app disclosures will be updated before that functionality is introduced.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Chitram does not retain photographs or GPS information on our servers unless explicitly stated for a particular feature.

Photographs stored on your device remain under your control and can be deleted using your device's gallery/file-management tools or through Chitram where supported.

If Chitram ever stores personal information on a server for an optional feature, we will provide appropriate information about the data retained, the retention period, and available deletion options.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Chitram.

Because photographs and GPS information may be stored locally on your device, you should also use the security features provided by your device, such as a screen lock, encryption, or other available security controls.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children's Privacy

Chitram is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of creating user profiles or advertising.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

12. Third-Party Services

Chitram may use third-party services necessary to provide specific functionality, such as maps, address lookup, crash reporting, analytics, or other technical services.

If third-party services are included in a particular version of Chitram, their data practices will be disclosed where required and their respective privacy policies may apply.

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of third-party services that they interact with.

13. Advertising

Chitram does not sell personal information for advertising purposes.

If advertising or advertising-related SDKs are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety information will be updated to accurately describe the relevant data practices.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Chitram's features, technology, or data practices change.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

We encourage you to periodically review this Privacy Policy.

15. Your Choices

You can:

  • Deny camera permission
  • Deny location permission
  • Control photo/media access
  • Revoke permissions through Android settings
  • Delete photographs stored on your device
  • Stop using the application at any time

Some Chitram features may not work when the corresponding permission is denied.
