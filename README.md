# సన్నివేశం (Sannivesham) 🪔

> **సంస్కృతి • సంప్రదాయం • సంభాషణ**  
> A modern, comprehensive digital sanctuary celebrating Telugu language, literature, Sanatana Dharma, Indian heritage, and sacred knowledge.

---

## ✨ Features & Modules

- **గ్రంథాలయం (Sacred Digital Library)**: Puranas, itihasas, slokas, stotrams, and timeless classical Telugu literature with interactive audio synchronization.
- **మేధ AI (Medha AI Assistant)**: Specialized cultural AI assistant providing respectful, grounded insights into Telugu literature, traditions, and scriptures.
- **పండుగలు (Festivals Guide)**: Complete calendar of Hindu and Telugu festivals, rituals, significance, and celebrations.
- **దేవాలయాలు (Temples Directory)**: Historical, architectural, and spiritual documentation of revered Indian pilgrimage sites and shrines.
- **క్యాలెండర్ & పంచాంగం (Panchangam & Calendar)**: Precise astronomical calculations for Tithi, Nakshatram, Rahu Kalam, and auspicious timings.
- **ప్రశ్నావళి (Cultural Quiz & Leaderboard)**: Daily Question of the Day (QOTD), difficulty-based quizzes, category challenges, and live competitive leaderboards.
- **అక్షరం (Aksharam Learning Engine)**: Interactive Telugu alphabet, vocabulary, pronunciation, and writing lessons for learners.
- **దృశ్యం (Drushyam Video Platform)**: Curated spiritual discourses, devotional chants, and documentary videos.
- **ఆటలు (Cultural Games)**: Telugu Word Search, Chitravela drawing, Sudoku, Mazes, Connect-the-Dots, and Sliding Puzzles.
- **పూజా మందిరం & జప గణన (Pooja Room & Chant Counter)**: Virtual prayer sanctuary with sacred temple sounds and interactive japa mala counter.
- **డెస్క్‌టాప్ యాప్ (Windows Desktop App)**: Electron-based offline-capable desktop application for Windows PCs and laptops.

---

## 🚀 Technology Stack

- **Frontend**: Vanilla HTML5, Modern CSS3 (Variables, CSS Grid, Flexbox, Responsive Design), Vanilla Modular JavaScript (ES6+).
- **Backend & Database**: Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database, Cloud Functions).
- **Search & Performance**: Responsive static delivery via GitHub Pages / CDN, smart client-side caching, and lightweight assets.
